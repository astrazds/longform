Longform

Capture the whole page.

Save a full-page screenshot or copy it to your clipboard. Free for Chrome. Every capture stays on your device.

Install in Chrome

  1. Download Longform and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and enable Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.

Then open a web page and choose Save or Copy in Longform.

What can I capture?

Longform works on standard http:// and https:// pages. Chrome blocks extensions on internal pages, extension pages, and other restricted pages.

Very large pages can exceed Chrome's image limits. Longform stops instead of saving a partial image. Copy also depends on browser support for clipboard images, so keep the popup open until it finishes.
